# Addendum (30 Sep 2026): Georgian SME accounting/ERP software landscape

Follow-up to the tech_digital.md gap on SME software adoption. All from search-result extracts; balance.ge itself is blocked from this environment.

## Balance (balance.ge)
- Developer: BDO Solutions LTD (BDO Georgia); launched 2016; claims first cloud-based accounting software in Georgia — [EUGBC](https://eugbc.net/219596/), [Gegidze](https://www.gegidze.com/post/digital-bookkeeping-tools-and-accounting-software-best-suited-for-georgian-companies)
- Scope: accounting, sales, warehouse, production, payroll, rs.ge sync, 60+ reports; cloud and on-premise — [support.balance.ge](https://support.balance.ge/)
- Pricing: Enterprise cloud licence GEL 79/month (GEL 59 first year via Bank of Georgia "Accounting Development Program"); onboarding GEL 760/day (GEL 500 for BOG participants); training GEL 350 (GEL 280) — [balance.ge/en/bog-partnership](https://balance.ge/en/bog-partnership)
- Distribution: Bank of Georgia partnership.

## Other incumbents
- Oris (oris.ge): "most popular accounting program in Georgia", rs.ge integrated, described as not fully meeting modern needs — [Jara Accounting](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/accounting-programs-georgia-jara-accounting)
- Fina (fina.ge): founded 2011, >5,000 customers, vertical modules (retail, cafes/restaurants, hotels, production, distribution, pharmacy); retail version ~GEL 80/month/outlet; bank and rs.ge integration; on-prem or cloud — [Fina about](https://fina.ge/about/en), [Jara Accounting](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/accounting-programs-georgia-jara-accounting)
- Apex ERP (apex.ge): cloud ERP with inventory, purchasing, sales, POS, accounting, HR — [Apex](https://www.apex.ge/en/epr/accounting)
- Restaurant POS: Poster POS has a Tbilisi presence; Syrve/Smaster and POS Solutions also active — [Poster Georgia FB](https://www.facebook.com/PosterSakartvelo/), [smaster.ge](https://smaster.ge/)

## Adoption (the key number)
- ISET Policy Institute, "SME Digitalization in Georgia: 2024 snapshot" (Aug 2025): only 6-7% of small firms use ERP and 3% CRM, vs 68% / 41% among large Georgian firms; EU SME ERP use 42%; AI use 2% small vs 20% large; only 7% of Georgian SMEs have websites with advanced features vs 78% in EU — [FREE Network brief](https://freepolicybriefs.org/2025/09/26/georgias-sme-digitalization-lags-behind-eu-despite-it-growth/), [ISET PDF](https://iset-pi.ge/storage/media/other/2025-09-15/d14138b0-9234-11f0-9205-a3ee28231f11.pdf)

## Inference
Supply of generic accounting/ERP is mature (four local vendors, ~GEL 80/month, bank channel). Low adoption is a demand/format problem (accountant-centric desktop tools, paid onboarding), not a supply gap. Opportunity is owner-facing, phone-first operational tools that integrate with incumbents, or tools sold to freelance accountants as the channel.
