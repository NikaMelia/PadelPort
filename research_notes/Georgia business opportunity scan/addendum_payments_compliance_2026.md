# Addendum (30 Sep 2026): Payments orchestration and compliance tooling check

Search-result extracts only.

## Orchestration already occupied
- 13 live gateways (BOG, TBC, Credo, Keepz, Flitt, UniPay, Liberty Card, ...) across cards, instalments, BNPL, QR, open banking, crypto — [Quickpay comparison](https://quickpay.ge/en/blog/georgian-payment-gateways-comparison)
- Keepz: first Open Banking licence in Georgia; evolved from QR tipping into a full payment orchestration platform (instant bank transfers, merchant acceptance, open banking, crypto tax collection) — [ecosystem.az](https://ecosystem.az/news/note/keepz-the-georgian-fintech-reshaping-how-a-country-pays)
- Quickpay.ge is a gateway aggregator; OMNO sells payment orchestration to online merchants — [Quickpay](https://quickpay.ge/en), [ensun list](https://ensun.io/search/payment-gateway/georgia)

## Regulated population and obligations
- 40 VASPs supervised by NBG (2026); named examples Bybit Georgia, City Pay, Coinmania, Digital Ledger Technologies — [Georgia Today](https://georgiatoday.ge/nbg-orders-virtual-asset-firms-to-show-registration-proof/), [thebanks.eu list](https://thebanks.eu/list-of-vasps/Georgia)
- ~60+ fintechs registered as PSPs; PSP registration ≥60 days; EMI capital GEL 1m — [Georgia.org fintech](https://georgia.org/industries/technology/fintech), [PB Services](https://pbservices.ge/blog/registering-payment-system-in-georgia/)
- VASP obligations: CDD on occasional transactions from USD/EUR 1,000 or GEL 3,000; must display NBG registration act on all channels from 1 Jan 2026; stablecoin issuers need GEL 500k segregated capital — [Andersen](https://ge.andersen.com/risk-assessments-vasps/), [PB Services stablecoin law](https://pbservices.ge/blog/stablecoins-new-law-georgia/)
- All obliged entities must run a software system to detect suspicious transactions and report to the Financial Monitoring Service — [Legal.ge AML guide](https://legal.ge/en/service/technology-and-digital-law-en/anti-money-laundering-compliance-en), [FMS Order N1](https://www.insurance.gov.ge/files/ShowFiles?id=1f1f3bad-8bea-4781-9d80-3326359d90f1)
- KYC vendors in use: Sumsub, Veriff, Ondato, Chainalysis — [Gegidze FATF guide](https://www.gegidze.com/post/how-to-stay-compliant-with-fatf-rules-as-a-georgian-crypto-business)
- No Georgia-specific regtech vendor surfaced in search.

## Instant Payment System (IPS)
- NBG + World Bank; public/business availability H2 2026; 24/7/365 A2A, QR, request-to-pay; banks and non-bank PSPs participate; NBG denies Visa/Mastercard restriction rumours — [NBG project](https://nbg.gov.ge/en/page/project), [Interfax](https://interfax.com/newsroom/top-stories/110659/), [JAMnews](https://jam-news.net/georgias-national-bank-on-new-payment-system/)
- Business implications: no cut-off times, instant payouts to couriers/drivers/sellers, A2A alternative to card acquiring, reconciliation changes — [Espero.ge](https://espero.ge/articles/en/instant-payments-in-georgia-ips-what-is-changing-for-businesses-and-when-to-expect-the-launch.html), [Payments Laboratory](https://paymentslaboratory.com/how-georgias-instant-payment-system-ips-can-redefine-the-ecosystem/)
- NBG also modernised RTGS with Montran (2026) — [Montran](https://www.montran.com/resources/national-bank-of-georgia-modernized-rtgs-with-montran)

## Revised inference
Orchestration: closed (Keepz, Quickpay, OMNO). Compliance tooling: ~100 small regulated firms; Georgian layer (monitoring rules, FMS report formats, NBG returns, local ID/registry checks) unserved by global KYC vendors; TAM ~GEL 0.6-2.4m/yr; needs compliance-domain co-founder. Strongest variant: IPS reconciliation and payout layer for merchants and accounting vendors (Balance, Fina, Oris) timed to the H2 2026 launch, merging with idea 2.
