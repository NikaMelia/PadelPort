## Overlap assessment
- Covers: acceptance, orchestration across methods, instant A2A rails, instant settlement. Confirms orchestration is closed; NBG IPS (H2 2026) will standardise rails Keepz/BoG already offer.
- Not found in any source: invoice/waybill matching, accounting sync (Balance/Fina/Oris), rs.ge sync, accountant/owner reporting, scheduled payout automation with bookkeeping.
- Risks: Keepz could add accounting sync quickly; Georgian fintech rails favour regulator insiders.
- Positioning: build the reconciliation/accounting layer on top of Keepz + bank gateways + NBG IPS; Keepz merchants are the first prospect list and Keepz a potential distribution partner.
