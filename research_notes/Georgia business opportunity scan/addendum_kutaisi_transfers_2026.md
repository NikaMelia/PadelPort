## Revised inference
Budget transfer need is met by USD 12 shuttles; tours are commoditised on GetYourGuide/Viator. Residual niche: fixed-price private/small-group transfers, night-arrival packages (pickup + airport-adjacent bed + onward transfer), luggage storage, "Kutaisi stopover" Imereti product. Ops-heavy, seasonal, Bolt price floor; a lifestyle business (USD 10-30k, 1-2 vans), not a scalable one. Deprioritised for a software-skilled founder.
