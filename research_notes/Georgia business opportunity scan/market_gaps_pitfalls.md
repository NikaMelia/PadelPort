# Georgia (country): Bottom-up Market Gaps, Demand Signals and Small-Business Pitfalls (2025-2026)

**Method note for the report writer.** The research environment blocked direct page fetches for nearly every Georgian and international outlet (georgiatoday.ge, bm.ge, civil.ge, forbes.ge, commersant.ge, oc-media.org, rferl.org, voxeurop.eu, nomads.com, expatexchange.com, tripadvisor, geostat.ge, wikipedia and others returned "egress blocked"), and the web-search budget was exhausted before every key question could be exhausted. Consequently, every finding below rests on search-engine result snippets/summaries of the cited page, not on a full read of the page. Numbers are reproduced as they appeared in those snippets; the writer should treat them as "reported by [source]" rather than independently verified, and should be aware that quotes may be paraphrased. Where a theme is supported by only one snippet I say so. "Anecdotal" = forum/blog/review sentiment; "surveyed/statistical" = Geostat, BAG index, Galt & Taggart, Colliers or similar.

## KQ1. What locals, expats and relocants repeatedly say is lacking or poor (Tbilisi, Batumi, Kutaisi, regions)

### Takeaway
The recurring, multi-source complaints are (1) dangerous driving culture and the pedestrian environment, (2) air pollution/noise/lack of parks in central Tbilisi, (3) fragmented healthcare where "English-speaking" is unreliable, (4) uneven quality of "international" schools, (5) airport taxi overcharging, and (6) post-2022 housing-cost shock and social friction around Russian-speaking newcomers. I could not access Reddit, Facebook, Telegram, Expat.com or InterNations threads directly, so classic "what's missing" items the brief asked about (parking, product returns, handymen, veterinary, kids' services, delivery quality) are NOT evidenced here and are listed as gaps.

### Cited Findings

**Driving and pedestrian safety (anecdotal; 4+ independent expat/nomad sources agree)**
- Nomad reviewer: "The driving style. Traffic is dangerous - people drive like maniac. Georgia has the highest death rate in Europe." — [Nomads.com Tbilisi reviews](https://nomads.com/reviews/tbilisi)
- "Traffic and driving culture frustrate many newcomers. Drivers don't use turn signals, tailgate constantly, run red lights, and weave through traffic while texting. Pedestrian crossings feel like suggestions rather than rules." — [Your New Base, Tbilisi Digital Nomad Guide (2026)](https://www.yournewbase.com/guides/tbilisi/); same theme in [Road Is Calling: living in Tbilisi pros/cons](https://www.roadiscalling.com/live-in-tbilisi-georgia/) and [Nomad Flag Tbilisi guide](https://nomadflag.com/tbilisi-georgia/)
- Counterpoint that keeps people there: "Drivers are aggressive but taxis are cheaper than anywhere I've ever been to (about 2usd for a 15 minute ride) ... on Bolt or Yandex apps." — [Nomads.com Tbilisi reviews](https://nomads.com/reviews/tbilisi)
- Georgia's own driving-guide sites flag "poor lighting outside city centers, uneven pavements, livestock on roads... tourism safety standards are uneven with first responders struggling outside major cities." — [Georgia Guidebook: Is Georgia Safe in 2026?](https://georgiaguidebook.com/planning/is-georgia-safe/)

**Air quality, noise, liveability (anecdotal; 3 sources)**
- Tbilisi "ranks one of the worst in Europe for tiny solid particulate matter (PM10 and PM2.5)... The majority of the pollution in Tbilisi comes from car exhausts." — [Nomads.com Pros and Cons of Tbilisi](https://nomads.com/pros-cons/tbilisi)
- "The city lacks liveability (parks, healthy food, noisy, polluted), with the downtown area not being ideal." — [Nomads.com Pros and Cons of Tbilisi](https://nomads.com/pros-cons/tbilisi); also raised in [Expat Exchange: Pros and Cons of Living in Tbilisi](https://www.expatexchange.com/gdc/6/46/4791/Georgia-rep/Pros-and-Cons-of-Living-in-Tbilisi)

**Healthcare access in English (anecdotal guide content; 3 sources)**
- "Finding a doctor who can take a history in English and then explain the result in it is the hard part." — [ExpatTools: Healthcare in Tbilisi 2026](https://expattools.org/city/tbilisi/healthcare.html)
- "The system is fragmented, with one clinic good for diagnostics, another better for surgery, and 'English-speaking' can mean anything from fluent doctor to receptionist confusion." Private clinics are modern and allow self-referral to any specialist. — [Georgia Expats: Healthcare guide (2026)](https://georgiaexpats.com/guides/healthcare/)
- Clinic-ranking content for "English-speaking doctors in Tbilisi" exists as a niche (8 clinics ranked), indicating demand for navigation/concierge. — [Tbilisi Experts: English-speaking doctors](https://tbilisiexperts.ge/best-english-speaking-doctors-tbilisi/)

**Schools and family services (anecdotal; 1 source)**
- "Tbilisi has around 16 international schools, but they're not created equal - some are world-class institutions... while others are Georgian private schools that call themselves 'international' because they offer some English-language classes." Informal homeschool groups exist in Vake and Saburtalo; parents organise via the Facebook group "Tbilisi Expat Parents". — [Georgia Expats: Education & Schools guide (2026)](https://georgiaexpats.com/guides/education/)

**Housing cost shock and rent volatility (anecdotal + statistical)**
- Euronews (Dec 2024): after 2022, tens of thousands of Russians arrived, "sending apartment prices skyrocketing, with some friends seeing their rent jump from EUR 250 per month in 2021 to more than EUR 700." — [Euronews, Dec 24 2024](https://www.euronews.com/2024/12/24/ive-worked-remotely-all-over-the-world-tbilisi-in-georgia-is-the-most-welcoming-place-ive-)
- Statistical reversal: Galt & Taggart put average Tbilisi apartment rent at USD 9.3/m2 in May 2025, down 2% m/m and the lowest in two years, after an 11% plunge in March 2025. — [CBW.ge citing Galt & Taggart](https://cbw.ge/real-estate/tbilisi-rental-prices-expected-to-stabilize-at-9-10-per-square-meter-in-2025-galt-taggart); rents down ~11% YoY while sale prices rise — [BTU AI: Why are apartment rents declining in Tbilisi](https://btuai.ge/en/why-are-apartment-rents-declining-in-tbilisi-and-what-it-means-for-owners-and-investors/)

**Relocant (Russian/Belarusian) experience and local friction (journalistic; 4 sources)**
- Russians "complained of hostility from regular Georgians and bureaucratic roadblocks"; some faced "verbal abuse, banking discrimination, and challenges in finding accommodation, with some landlords openly refusing to rent to Russians." — [RFE/RL, Jan 2024](https://www.rferl.org/a/georgia-russians-fleeing-war-leaving-/32784835.html)
- "Many Georgians say that the prospect of encountering the unknown is enough to stop them going into Russian-owned establishments altogether." — [France 24, May 2024](https://www.france24.com/en/europe/20240516-in-georgia-russian-%C3%A9migr%C3%A9s-see-familiar-kremlin-tactics)
- Language politics around Russian in service settings documented in "Tongue-tied in Tbilisi" — [Novaya Gazeta Europe, Jul 2 2025](https://novayagazeta.eu/en/articles/2025/07/02/tongue-tied-in-tbilisi-en)
- "Numerous catering establishments have been opened by Russians, which mainly serve Russians and, in many cases, do not even pay taxes"; owners reset the one-year visa-free stay by briefly leaving. — [Jamestown Foundation: Russian companies winning state tenders in Georgia](https://jamestown.org/russian-companies-winning-state-tenders-in-georgia/)

**Airport taxi overcharging (anecdotal; multiple TripAdvisor posters + 2 guide sites)**
- "Every single taxi driver approaching you at arrivals or outside is going to cheat and overcharge you!... The police and other authorities in Tbilisi including the tourist authority definitely know about it but haven't done anything." One poster paid 280 GEL airport to Ibis Tbilisi Stadium; Bolt costs ~25 GEL. — [TripAdvisor Tbilisi forum: Scammed taxi report](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/ShowTopic-g294195-i9344-k14920782-Scammed_taxi_or_Illegal_taxi_driver_report-Tbilisi.html); guides: [Tbilisi Expat taxi guide](https://tbilisi-expat.com/blog/tbilisi-taxi-guide), [Wander-Lush taxi guide](https://wander-lush.org/taxi-tbilisi/)

**Food delivery (market structure; 1 self-published source, low confidence)**
- Tbilisi food delivery in 2026 "is dominated by Wolt, with Glovo, Bolt Food, and GeraEats also operating"; Bolt Food "re-entered Georgia in 2024". — [GeraEats blog](https://geraeats.com/blog/food-delivery-tbilisi) (a competitor's own blog; treat as low-confidence)
- Couriers for Glovo, Wolt and Bolt Food have repeatedly struck and vowed to form a gig-economy union (2021 reporting). — [OC Media: couriers vow gig-economy union](https://oc-media.org/georgian-couriers-vow-to-create-gig-economy-union-following-glovo-strike/); [OC Media: Bolt Food couriers strike](https://oc-media.org/bolt-food-couriers-on-strike-in-tbilisi/)

### Inferences
- The strongest convergent demand signals from residents are for: pedestrian/child-safe environments and indoor family leisure (given driving and pollution complaints); trusted, English-first medical navigation or concierge (fragmented clinics, unreliable "English-speaking" claims); transparent, vetted international schooling; and fixed-price airport transfer products. None of these are proven by survey data here, only by repeated anecdote.
- Rent volatility (sharp 2022-23 spike, 11% fall in 2025) means any Tbilisi business model that locked in 2023-era leases is now over-paying relative to the market, and landlords may be more negotiable in 2025-26 than at any time since 2021 (inference from G&T data).
- The relocant boom created businesses that served a Russian-speaking bubble; social friction plus the 2023-24 outflow makes that a shrinking customer base (see KQ4).

### Gaps
- No direct access to r/Tbilisi, r/Sakartvelo, Facebook expat groups, Telegram relocant channels, Expat.com or InterNations; the search engine surfaced no indexed Reddit threads. Themes the brief asked about (parking, public transport quality, product returns, handymen/trades quality, veterinary, kids' services, specific foods missing, customer service norms) are therefore unevidenced here.
- No Batumi- or Kutaisi-specific resident complaint threads were found (only tourist-facing content, see KQ2).
- No Ukrainian, Turkish, Indian, Iranian, Israeli or Western-community-specific complaint sources surfaced.

## KQ2. What tourists say is missing or disappointing (Tbilisi, Batumi, Kazbegi, Svaneti, Kakheti, Gudauri/Bakuriani)

### Takeaway
Tourist disappointment clusters around Batumi's loss of Georgian character, airport taxi scams in Tbilisi, weather/road fragility in the mountains (Kazbegi, Svaneti), and resort-town infrastructure that has not kept pace with visitor volume (Bakuriani traffic and sidewalks, Mestia's overloaded grid and unpermitted concrete guesthouses). Statistical sources conflict on whether 2025 was a boom or a slump: GNTA-style aggregates show record visits, while the hotel federation says Tbilisi room rates hit a 30-year low.

### Cited Findings

**Batumi (anecdotal; 3 sources)**
- "Visitors looking for Georgian food, music and a taste of local culture are finding it increasingly difficult to find in Batumi." — [Georgia Today: Are Batumi's challenges made worse by locals?](https://georgiatoday.ge/are-batumis-challenges-made-worse-not-by-tourists-but-by-locals/)
- A travel blogger found Batumi disappointing and "would skip it if doing their Georgia trip again." — [Indie Traveller: Is Batumi worth visiting?](https://www.indietraveller.co/batumi-not-my-favorite-place/)
- "Batumi in July and August is entirely tourist-dominated, largely Turkish, Israeli, Ukrainian, and Russian." — [Georgia Spirit: Tbilisi vs Batumi 2026](https://www.georgia-spirit.com/guides/tbilisi-vs-batumi/)
- Casino guest complaints: an Israeli visitor found Otium Casino's atmosphere "tense and unwelcoming, especially for tourists"; another reported waitresses so slow that orders took up to 20 minutes. — [TripAdvisor: Batumi casinos](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Attractions-g297576-Activities-c53-t107-Batumi_Adjara_Region.html)
- Seasonality: "Batumi casinos are bracing for a seasonal downturn, with the slot hall Atlantic in Batumi closed until October." Turkish Black Sea players dominate, Israelis "a strong second". — [iGB: Black Sea Vegas Batumi rides Israeli wave](https://igamingbusiness.com/casino/integrated-resorts/black-sea-vegas-batumi-rides-israeli-wave/)

**Tbilisi (anecdotal)**
- Airport taxi overcharging and Old Town tourist traps (currency confusion, inflated prices) — [TripAdvisor forum](https://www.tripadvisor.co.uk/ShowTopic-g294195-i9344-k14920782-Scammed_taxi_or_Illegal_taxi_driver_report-Tbilisi.html); [Tbilisi Expat: scams to avoid](https://tbilisi-expat.com/blog/tbilisi-scams-to-avoid)

**Kazbegi / Georgian Military Highway (guide content)**
- Road "often has snow... black ice, packed snow, and limited visibility"; "the route from Mestia to Ushguli in Svaneti is effectively impassable in deep winter." — [OG.ge: Driving in Georgia 2026](https://og.ge/blog/driving-in-georgia)

**Svaneti (mixed: statistical on electricity, anecdotal on crowding)**
- Mestia's annual electricity consumption rose from 91.1 million kWh (2020) to 132.9 million kWh (2025); "illegal connections and heavy demand from crypto mining... pushing parts of the system into a critical condition"; Vice PM Mdinaradze said every Georgian consumer effectively pays an extra GEL 1.5 per bill to cover Svaneti's excess consumption. — [BM.ge: Mestia electricity up 45% over five years](https://bm.ge/en/news/mestias-electricity-consumption-up-by-45-over-five-years-amid-crypto-mining-concerns)
- "Most tourists go to Mestia (2,700 inhabitants) and hike/drive east towards Ushguli (200 inhabitants) between June-September... New concrete guesthouses are being built randomly, without permission, and are incompatible with the local cultural and architectural heritage." — [Green Global Travel: effects of mass tourism](https://greenglobaltravel.com/effects-mass-tourism-overtourism-destroying-destinations/)
- Flights to Mestia "operate under visual flight rules and depend on actual weather... making weather-related cancellation an explicit operational feature." — [Travel and Tour World: Svaneti access strain](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/o0q2doz842rq/)
- Positive infrastructure change: the Zagari Pass road to Ushguli "has been almost entirely paved or concreted as of 2025." — [Wander-Lush: New in Georgia 2025](https://wander-lush.org/new-in-georgia-2025/)

**Gudauri (anecdotal guide content; 3 sources agree queues are modest)**
- "There are no 40-minute queues here like at European resorts"; weekdays "practically empty" but "skiing tracks are crowded at the weekends, and there are long queues at the cashier's office." Three new lifts (New Gudaura, Kikilo 1, Kikilo 2; 6 km) added after Dec 2023. — [Madloba Gudauri guide](https://madloba.info/en/blog/georgian-cities/gudauri/); [Wander-Lush: 18 things to know about Gudauri](https://wander-lush.org/visit-gudauri-travel-tips/); [Intrepid Escape: Skiing Gudauri 2026](https://www.intrepidescape.com/skiing-gudauri-georgia/)

**Bakuriani (industry complaint; 1 source)**
- Hills Hotel management: "This year, Bakuriani met the tourist season especially unprepared" - hourly traffic jams on New Year's Eve; "there are almost no sidewalks in Bakuriani"; problems with road salting "puts vacationers at risk". — [Commersant: Bakuriani unprepared - Hills Hotel](https://commersant.ge/en/news/business/this-year-bakuriani-met-the-tourist-season-especially-unprepared-hills-hotel)
- World Bank (May 2024): winter tourism suffers from "inadequate basic infrastructure (roads) or the absence of a comprehensive mobility plan." — [World Bank: Georgia Tourism Trends Analysis & Recommendations](https://documents1.worldbank.org/curated/en/099060424183542556/pdf/P5016461c0c9e502818eb419dba3272441a.pdf)

**Kutaisi / Imereti (academic + guide)**
- "The infrastructure of the Imereti region, including roads and hotels, does not meet international standards in the field of tourism." — [Georgian Scientists journal: place marketing in Imereti/Kutaisi](https://journals.4science.ge/index.php/GS/article/view/3234)
- "There are no hotels at Kutaisi Airport, but you will find a few options in Samtredia (16 minutes away)." — [Sleeping in Airports: Kutaisi](https://www.sleepinginairports.net/guides/kutaisi-airport-guide.htm)

**Macro tourism picture 2025 (statistical, conflicting)**
- ~6.9 million international visits in 2025 (+6.2%), ~USD 4.69 billion revenue (+6%); hotel occupancy in Tbilisi, Batumi and Gudauri averaged 76%; investment diversifying to Kakheti, Gudauri, Svaneti, Borjomi, Samegrelo. — [Rus Tourism News, Jun 2026](https://www.rustourismnews.com/2026/06/12/georgia-strengthens-its-position-as-a-leading-caucasus-tourism-destination/); 8.2 million total visitors claimed by [Caucasus Business Journal](https://caucasusbusinessjournal.com/news/georgia-tourism-8-2-million-visitors)
- Contradicted by industry: hotel co-founder Shalva Alaverdashvili said it was "humiliating for the sector to claim a record income when, in reality, hotels are obliged to lower their prices to their lowest in recent years"; "prices haven't been so low in Tbilisi for 30 years"; tourism revenue down 35% YoY in Dec 2024 and 38% in Jan 2025; European visitors scared off by political turmoil. — [Voxeurop, Jun 2025](https://voxeurop.eu/en/georgia-businesses-crisis-russia/)
- Q3 2025: "both the average cost of tourist services and the number of domestic tourists changed for the slightly worse." — [Travel and Tour World: tourism slowing](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/tourism-in-georgia-shows-signs-of-slowing-but-tbilisi-and-beyond-still-attract-tourists/)

### Inferences
- Batumi has a documented "authenticity gap": a Georgian-culture product (food, music, crafts) aimed at the Turkish/Israeli/CIS summer crowd is repeatedly described as missing.
- Mountain resorts show demand outrunning infrastructure (Bakuriani traffic/sidewalks, Mestia grid, Kutaisi hotels). Businesses that solve last-mile or resilience problems (shuttles, backup power, winter-ready transfers, snow services) address complaints that locals and hoteliers themselves voice.
- The visitor mix shifted away from Europeans in 2024-25 (per Voxeurop) toward regional/CIS and Gulf/Israeli markets; a business dependent on Western tourists faces political-risk-driven volatility.

### Gaps
- Could not read TripAdvisor forum threads or Google review patterns directly; Kakheti and Kazbegi tourist-complaint content did not surface at all (only marketing and guide pages).
- No source quantified visitor complaints (e.g., review-score distributions) for any destination.

## KQ3. What Georgian small-business owners say is hardest (staff, rent, landlords, bureaucracy, tax, lending, seasonality, copying, corruption)

### Takeaway
The only rigorous survey surfaced is the Business Association of Georgia (BAG) index: in Q1 2026, 55% of companies named the shortage of specialists their top problem, most acutely in construction and services, with legal/administrative barriers and financing next. Hospitality operators describe 2024-25 as a crisis of collapsing prices and layoffs driven by political turmoil. Rent pressure has eased (residential rents down 11% in 2025), and 2026 brings a new compliance burden: mandatory work permits for foreign staff with inspections and fines. Evidence on landlord practices, tax audits, bank lending terms, copying and corruption was NOT found.

### Cited Findings

**Surveyed evidence (BAG Index, Q1 2026)**
- "55% of surveyed companies continued to cite the shortage of specialists as their primary challenge, though this figure decreased by 10 percentage points compared to the previous quarter. Across sectors, the shortage of specialists is most acutely felt in construction and the service industry." Concerns about legal/administrative barriers and labor shortages fell 14 and 13 percentage points respectively; "for the construction and trade sectors, legal and administrative barriers, as well as limited financial resources, remain significant challenges." 65% expect to raise investment in 2026 (manufacturing 81%, services/trade 61%, construction 60%). — [Sova News, May 1 2026: BAG survey](https://sovanews.tv/en/2026/05/01/bag-survey-specialist-shortage-remains-top-challenge-for-business/); index home: [BAG Index](https://bag.ge/en/bag-index)
- Forbes Georgia frames the climate as "Economic Growth Faces Political, Workforce and Structural Barriers" (article body not readable). — [Forbes Georgia](https://forbes.ge/en/georgia-s-business-landscape-economic-growth-faces-political-workforce-and-structural-barriers/)

**Hospitality operators (journalistic, 2025)**
- "Georgia's hospitality sector faces closures, layoffs and plummeting prices - as political turmoil and uncertainty scare away European visitors and stall local recovery." Capital hotels "in the worst possible situation... resulted in staff numbers being reduced." Example closure: Bushmala, a fast-food cafe on Simon Chikovani Street run by siblings who had won a culinary show. — [Voxeurop, Jun 2025](https://voxeurop.eu/en/georgia-businesses-crisis-russia/)
- Political discontent since spring 2024 "permeated into all aspects of life in Tbilisi - including the culinary." — [Culinary Backstreets: A slow restart for Tbilisi's restaurants and bars](https://culinarybackstreets.com/stories/tbilisi/notes-on-reopening-6)

**Rent and property costs (statistical)**
- Residential rent Tbilisi USD 9.3/m2 May 2025, lowest in two years; G&T expected stabilisation at USD 9-10/m2. — [CBW.ge / Galt & Taggart](https://cbw.ge/real-estate/tbilisi-rental-prices-expected-to-stabilize-at-9-10-per-square-meter-in-2025-galt-taggart)
- Gross rental yield in Tbilisi fell to 7.78% in Q1 2025 from over 9% a year earlier. — [Global Property Guide: Georgia rental yields](https://www.globalpropertyguide.com/asia/georgia/rental-yields)
- Galt & Taggart (Apr 2025) expected demand to soften "due to domestic political instability, with a projected slowdown in economic growth, GEL depreciation, and potential reduction of rental yields"; "weakening demand from migrant buyers, who previously played a notable role in driving rental and sale prices." — [Investor.ge, Apr 2025](https://www.investor.ge/2025/04/06/georgias-residential-real-estate-market-trends-challenges-and-prospects/)
- Sale prices kept rising: Tbilisi averages USD 1,484/m2 (new build) and USD 1,244/m2 (old stock); 42,267 Tbilisi transactions in 2025 (+3%), 17,052 in Batumi (+17%), USD 4.3bn combined. — [Georgia Today citing Colliers](https://georgiatoday.ge/tbilisi-and-batumi-apartment-sales-surpass-4-3-billion-in-2025/); [GBC.ge](https://www.gbc.ge/public/en/news/Economics-news/record-year-for-georgias-real-estate-market-43-billion-in-apartments-sold-in-tbilisi-and-batumi)
- Retail asking rents on listings portal: roughly USD 10-40/m2/month; Vake examples USD 14-23/m2, Saburtalo USD 10-40/m2 (listing snapshot, not a survey). — [Korter.ge retail for rent Tbilisi](https://korter.ge/en/retail-property-for-rent-tbilisi)
- Colliers retail tracker: shopping-centre vacancy ~9% (improved 1pp since 2019, absorption at City Mall Saburtalo, Gldani Mall, City Mall Gldani) - date of snapshot unclear. — [RECOV.ge by Colliers: Retail](http://old.recov.ge/en/sectors/retail/)

**Regulatory/compliance burden: 2026 labour-migration law (legal-advisory sources; 5 agree)**
- Parliament passed reforms in 2025 effective 1 March 2026; foreign nationals without permanent residence need a work permit; self-employed foreigners and remote workers employed by local employers also need a "right to work" from the State Employment Support Agency; exemptions for refugees, asylum seekers, diplomats, foreign journalists, investment-residence holders; Labour Inspectorate and MIA conduct on-site checks; obstructing inspections fined GEL 1,000-7,000 by company size; transition until 1 January 2027. — [Eurofast](https://eurofast.eu/georgias-2026-labour-migration-law-reforms-work-permits-digital-nomads-immigration-compliance/); [Fragomen](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/new-work-permit-rules-georgia-immigration.html); [US Embassy Georgia](https://ge.usembassy.gov/new-work-permit-rules-for-foreign-nationals-in-georgia-starting-march-1/); [OC Media explainer](https://oc-media.org/explainer-what-foreign-nationals-need-to-know-about-georgias-new-labour-laws/); [PB Services](https://pbservices.ge/blog/work-permits-to-become-mandatory-in-georgia-in-2026/)
- Enforcement already visible: "raids in Tbilisi and Batumi that included checks of food-service businesses and the work gear of couriers linked to delivery platforms." — [Caspian Post: Georgia's new migration enforcement strategy](https://caspianpost.com/analytics/what-georgia-s-new-migration-enforcement-strategy-means)

**Informal competition (journalistic; 2 sources)**
- Russian-run catering establishments serving Russians "in many cases, do not even pay taxes." — [Jamestown](https://jamestown.org/russian-companies-winning-state-tenders-in-georgia/)
- "Russian migrants rapidly reshaped Georgia's tourism, hospitality and IT sectors, displacing local businesses." — [Voxeurop: silent Russian take-over of key sectors](https://voxeurop.eu/en/russian-take-over-business-georgia/)

**Gig labour relations**
- Repeated courier strikes at Glovo, Wolt, Bolt Food (2021). — [OC Media](https://oc-media.org/georgian-couriers-vow-to-create-gig-economy-union-following-glovo-strike/)

### Inferences
- Staffing, not rent, is now the binding constraint for service SMEs (BAG data plus double-digit wage growth in KQ5). Any concept that is labour-light or that can train unskilled staff quickly has a structural advantage.
- Rents are the one cost that moved in operators' favour in 2025; leases signed in 2022-23 are likely above market.
- The 2026 permit regime raises the cost and risk of the common relocant model of hiring compatriots informally; inspections of food service and couriers suggest hospitality and delivery are early enforcement targets.
- Political risk is treated by operators as the primary demand shock of 2024-25 (European tourism fell while headline aggregates rose because regional markets grew).

### Gaps
- No evidence found on: landlord practices (deposits, indexation, evictions), tax-audit behaviour of the Revenue Service, Business Ombudsman complaint data, SME bank-lending rates/collateral, seasonality cash-flow management, competitor copying, or corruption as experienced by SMEs. Searches for these returned US-Georgia results or nothing.
- PMC Research, ISET, AmCham Georgia and EU4Business SME surveys for 2025 were not retrievable (AmCham search returned only Taiwan/China chapters).
- Commercial (retail/office) rent series from Colliers or Galt & Taggart for 2025 were not retrievable beyond listing snapshots.

## KQ4. Case studies: businesses started 2019-2025 that worked, and ones that failed or closed

### Takeaway
Documented successes cluster in specialty coffee (CoffeeLab, Shavi), padel (at least five Tbilisi clubs opened, an FIP tournament hosted in 2025), dental tourism (clinics processing thousands of foreign patients), international QSR franchises (Pizza Hut 2024, Starbucks 2026) and low-cost aviation feeding Kutaisi. The failure side is thin: the only named closure with a reason is Bushmala (hospitality downturn, 2025); the broader "failure" is the 2023-24 unwinding of the Russian-relocant economy (a third of arrivals gone, remittances down 78%). I could not assemble 5-8 named relocant-founded closures with documented reasons.

### Cited Findings

**Successes / survivors**
1. **CoffeeLab (Tbilisi, Saburtalo; opened 2016)** - "the first specialty roaster and cafe to enter the Georgian market"; founder Giorgi Aivazyan describes "the uphill battle... educating its customers during its first few years"; deliberately located outside touristic districts to build a loyal local base. — [Perfect Daily Grind, Jan 2026](https://perfectdailygrind.com/2026/01/georgia-specialty-coffee-market/)

## KQ5. Labour market: wages, staff availability, English-speaking staff, migrant labour rules, wage inflation 2022-2025

### Takeaway
Nominal wages grew 10-12% a year through 2025 (average GEL 2,466 in Q4 2025) while unemployment stayed at 13.9% and participation fell to 54.5%: a market where employers cannot find skilled staff yet a large pool is idle, i.e., a skills/attitude mismatch rather than a shortage of bodies. The statutory minimum wage remains GEL 20/month (unchanged since 1999). From March 2026 foreign staff need work permits, adding cost to the common practice of hiring relocants informally.

### Cited Findings

## KQ6. Demographics and social trends and what they imply for demand

### Takeaway
Georgia is shrinking naturally (births at a 32-year low, TFR 1.61, natural change -6,452 in 2025) and ageing, with natural growth only in Tbilisi, Adjara and Kvemo Kartli; headline population still edged up 0.3% to 3.94 million on 1 Jan 2026 because net migration (+17,127) was positive. Emigration nearly doubled in 2023. Implications: shrinking child/youth cohorts outside the capital, rising elderly-dependency, and continued dependence on inbound migrants and remittances.

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- Demand for elderly-care, home-care and chronic-disease services will rise in the regions where natural decline is steepest (everywhere outside Tbilisi, Adjara, Kvemo Kartli); demand for kids' services concentrates in the three growing regions.

- Remittance dependence is volatile (Russia corridor -78% in one year), so remittance-adjacent businesses need multi-corridor exposure (EU, US, Israel).
