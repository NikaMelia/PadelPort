# Addendum (30 Sep 2026): Batumi short-term rental and management market update

Search-result extracts only; primary pages not fetched.

## Unit economics deteriorating
- AirROI 2026: Batumi average annual revenue USD 3,702, ADR USD 57, occupancy 35.5%, RevPAR USD 21, 3,491 active listings; Tbilisi USD 5,972, ADR USD 58, occupancy 40.1%, 12,195 listings (May 2026) — [AirROI Batumi](https://www.airroi.com/airbnb-data/georgia/autonomous-republic-of-adjara/batumi), [AirROI Tbilisi](https://www.airroi.com/airbnb-data/georgia/tbilisi/tbilisi)
- Compare Airbtics Sep 2024-Aug 2025: USD ~8k/yr, 57% occupancy, ADR USD 43 — [Airbtics](https://airbtics.com/annual-airbnb-revenue-in-batumi-georgia/). Vendors differ in method; direction is down.

## Oversupply
- Unsold Batumi apartments ~12,400 at end-2025 (+13.9% y/y); yields compressed 8.8% (2024) to 7.4% (2025), forecast 5.1-3.4% as pipeline completes; several large premium launches in 2026 — [Galt & Taggart Batumi 2025 review / 2026 outlook](https://galtandtaggart.com/report/batumi-residential-real-estate-2025-review-and-2026-outlook/), [Investor.ge Apr 2026](https://www.investor.ge/2026/04/20/sector-snapshot-batumi-residential-real-estate/)

## Regulation
- Tbilisi/Batumi STR regulation profile "low", minimal registration — [AirROI Georgia](https://www.airroi.com/airbnb-data/georgia)

## Revised inference
"Be the manager" is a thin-margin ops business (~USD 900/unit/yr at 25% fee) against 200 incumbents; deprioritise. Surviving variants: (a) software for small managers (owner statements in RU/HE/EN, rs.ge invoicing, cleaning ops); (b) owner-side manager marketplace with verified performance, sold to absentee foreign investors and developers. Niche size is the limiting factor.
